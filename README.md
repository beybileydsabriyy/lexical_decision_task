# lexical_decision_task
It's a bilingual version of LDT, containing words from English and Turkish
The English non-words and real words are from "https://elexicon.wustl.edu/wordstart.html" 
There were no Turkish studies, so I created non-words and selected some Turkish words without verifying their parameters. 
There are 40 words in total, 20 Turkish words and 20 English words. These 20 are divided into two conditions: 10 non-words and 10 real words.
