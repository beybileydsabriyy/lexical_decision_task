I have developed a bilingual version of the Lexical Decision Task (LDT), which measures an individual's ability to distinguish real words from non-words. While this ability is typically measured via reaction time, EEG can also be integrated into such experiments to observe specific ERP components that become active during semantic processing.

For this variation, the task consists of two trial blocks containing both Turkish and English words to compare reaction times across languages. It's a really short task. 
Hope you enjoy it!
